About

I'm a passionate Software Engineer specializing in full-stack development and DevOps. My expertise bridges the front and back end, allowing me to deliver seamless, high-performing applications. With a strong foundation in modern frameworks and languages, I excel at creating intuitive, responsive user interfaces that work beautifully across devices, alongside robust, scalable back-end solutions that power them.

On the DevOps side, I'm experienced in automating deployments, managing infrastructure, and ensuring systems are reliable and secure. I believe in leveraging CI/CD pipelines, containerization, and cloud services to streamline development and create consistent, efficient workflows. My goal is always to optimize the development lifecycle, ensuring rapid, reliable delivery and a top-tier user experience..

Key skills include:

  • Full-Stack Development (MERN & PERN Stack)
  • Frontend Frameworks: React, Redux, Next.js
  • Backend Development: Node.js, Express, REST APIs, GraphQL
  • Database Management: MongoDB, PostgreSQL, MySQL
  • Cloud Services & Infrastructure: AWS (EC2, S3, RDS, Lambda)

  • Serverless Architecture & Microservices
  • Data Security & Authentication (OAuth, JWT)
  • Certifications: AWS Certified Solutions Architect, AWS Certified Developer

In every project, I’m driven by problem-solving, collaboration, and continuous learning to keep up with the latest in technology. I'm excited to bring both creativity and precision to the work I do, helping businesses turn their ideas into reality.
